# English Islamiat MCQ source review

The 102 questions in `Data/Class IX/Islamiat_MCQs.json` were transcribed against all 31 pages of the supplied `Data/Class IX/Islamiat_MCQs.pdf` exercise extract, not from OCR alone. Page references identify the extract and the printed book page. Chapter names follow the contents of a matching [English textbook copy](https://dae-note.com/9th-class-islamiat-book-sindh-board-english-medium-pdf/): The Holy Quran; The Blessed Hadith; Thematic Study; Pearls of Guidance and Personalities of Islam. The textbook itself is titled for Classes IX–X. An OCR-assisted scan of all 176 pages of the matching full copy found the exercise headings corresponding to the extract; OCR can miss text, so this is not an independent page-by-page proof of full-book coverage. The user confirmed that this material is all Class IX, despite the textbook’s IX–X title. The bank is available under IX and has not been duplicated into X. No answer key has been invented. Arabic text and vowel marks have not had an independent second proofread.

Five questions on printed page 167 have three choices as printed. On printed page 89, question 3 option d is truncated in both the supplied extract and the matching full-book copy: `great Jihad (Jihad-e-A`. The bank preserves the visible fragment and flags the truncation in the question; its completion is not inferred. That question may also have overlapping choices (big/great Jihad). On printed page 81 question 3, the source stem reads `The of human creation is:` and is retained. Printed page 70’s question 1 option b reads `to believe in`, retained as printed.

Some printed option labels are duplicated: printed page 140 question ii and page 143 question ii label the third choice b; printed page 147 questions ii and iii also repeat or misorder labels. These are represented in positional A–D order so all choices remain usable. On page 159 question ii, the source lays out labels in the order a, c, b, d; the bank retains the letter-to-choice mapping in A–D order. Questions and options otherwise retain source wording, with typographic spacing and punctuation normalized. The three-option questions remain three-option questions.
