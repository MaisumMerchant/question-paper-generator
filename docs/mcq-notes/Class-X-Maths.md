# Class X Mathematics MCQ source review

The supplied 25-page `Data/Class X/Math_MCQs.pdf` extract was compared with the 158 MCQs in source order, from Review Exercises 17–30. It is an exercise extract, not evidence of the complete textbook. No answer key was provided.

Eight figure-dependent questions in Exercises 25 and 26 use crops from source PDF pages 16 and 18. The diagrams are embedded in previews and PDF/Word exports, but the supplied scans are low resolution and watermarked. In particular, the values and labels in Exercise 25 (iii) are difficult to read; obtain a clearer original before relying on that question. The figures have not been redrawn or guessed.

Potential source ambiguities include Exercise 17 (i), Exercise 18 (xii), Exercise 28 (ix), and Exercise 29 (xi, xv), where more than one option may be correct depending on the interpretation. Source wording and apparent errors are retained; this comparison is not a mathematical validation of every answer.
