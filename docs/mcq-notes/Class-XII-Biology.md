# Class XII Biology MCQ source review

The 25-page `Data/Class XII/Biology_MCQs.pdf` supplies ten exercise MCQs for each chapter 15–27 (130 in total). The JSON transcribes those exercise questions, not the short- or long-answer questions. Chapter 17 question x was restored and a Chapter 16 option typo fixed. No answer key is asserted.

Some source questions contain scientific or editorial problems; wording and options are retained rather than silently inventing replacements:
- Chapter 15 question iii prints calcium as Ca⁺, though calcium ions are normally Ca²⁺.
- Chapter 23 question v asks for a boy with blood group O from two heterozygous group-A parents; the probability is 1/8, which is not among the printed options. Question x asks “more than” with multiple overlapping numerical choices.
- Chapter 25 question iv reads “All is positive” without identifying a quantity. Question ix says the sun is the only energy source for all ecosystems, overlooking chemosynthesis.
- Several Chapter 26 questions have potentially overlapping choices or broad wording (for example, monoclonal antibodies have diagnostic and therapeutic uses).
- Chapter 27 question x includes the answer in its stem as printed.

The source is an exercise extract; it does not establish the complete contents of a full textbook or other editions.
