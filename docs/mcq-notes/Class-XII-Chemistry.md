# Class XII Chemistry MCQ source review

The 130 questions (ten in each of 13 exercises) were compared with the 25-page `Data/Class XII/Chemistry_MCQs.pdf` exercise extract. This is not evidence of complete textbook coverage. No answer key was supplied. Chapter 7–9 and 13 labels were separated from incorrect Organic Compounds and Environmental Chemistry labels; topic titles are consistent with the [Class XII syllabus](https://zueb.edu.pk/assets/images/resource/exam_material_25/grade_12/HSSC-II%20Chemistry.pdf).

Corrections include the alkene options in Organic Compounds (ii), the structure and option text in Nomenclature (iii, viii, ix), Hydrocarbons (i–ii), the printed amine geometry in Alkyl Halides and Amines (vi), the phosphorus reagent in Alcohols (iii), and several Aldehydes/Ketones and Carboxylic Acids choices. Original apparent typos are retained, including “steric acid”, “1,2-bromo ethane”, “Potassium dichromatic” and “Dihyderic alcohol”.

Several textbook questions appear ambiguous or defective; their wording and choices remain as printed, with no answer asserted:
- Nomenclature (ii) has more than one possible class for C₇H₁₂; (ix)(a) prints a questionable structure.
- Hydrocarbons (i) does not specify the amount of HBr; (x) does not identify the acylating reagent.
- Alkyl Halides and Amines (iv) does not list the usual final alcohol product of an ester and Grignard reagent; (ix) lists no obvious reagent for making propane from methylmagnesium iodide.
- Alcohols (ix) describes secondary alcohol oxidation to a carboxylic acid via an intermediate, a problematic generalization.
- Biochemistry (ix) does not state which set of alpha amino acids is being counted; 20 standard proteinogenic amino acids are not offered.
- Environmental Chemistry (vi) presents overlapping green chemistry aims; acid rain (ii) omits values between pH 5 and 5.6.

This review checks transcription against the supplied pages, not the scientific validity of every item. Other original wording, spelling, and apparent printing mistakes have not been silently repaired.
