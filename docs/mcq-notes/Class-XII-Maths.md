# Class XII Mathematics MCQ review

The supplied `Data/Class XII/Math_MCQs.pdf` is an exercise-page extract, not the full textbook. The questions in Functions and Limits, Differentiation, Higher Order Derivatives and Applications, and Differentiation of Vector Functions were re-transcribed against the extract (review exercises 2–5, 56 questions). The subsequent sections document the later review exercises.

Some printed questions are ambiguous or flawed: review exercise 2(iv) gives a relation but does not state what to find, and 2(xii) asks for a two-sided limit that does not exist among the given choices. Review exercise 3(xi) depends on the interval of x. Review exercise 4(ix) can depend on whether decreasing means strictly or non-strictly, and 4(x) has related stationary/critical terminology. These are retained as printed, without an answer key.

## Review exercises 6–7

The 20 Integration questions (PDF pages 10–13, printed pages 188–191) and 10 Plane Analytic Geometry: Straight Line questions (PDF pages 14–15, printed pages 238–239) were transcribed from the page images. This brought the reviewed portion to 86 questions. The supplied extract begins at Review Exercise 2; see the Unit 1 comparison below.

Printed issues retained without an answer key: 6(iii) writes ln x rather than ln |x|; 6(xi) involves inverse cosecant conventions and domain/sign restrictions; 6(xvi) does not explicitly identify the other boundary of the area; 6(xx) does not state restrictions on a. In 7(iii)–(iv), slope criteria as printed do not address vertical lines. 7(iii) option (c) says “Does not exit” in the source.

## Review exercise 8

The 20 Circle questions were transcribed from PDF pages 16–17 (printed pages 272–273), bringing the reviewed portion to 106. In 8(i), “perpendicularly” does not specify the reference direction. In 8(xiii), the printed equation has no real points, so it does not define a real circle; the printed question and options are retained without an answer key.

## Review exercise 9

The 20 Parabola, Ellipse and Hyperbola questions were transcribed from PDF pages 18–20 (printed pages 348–350). In 9(iv), options (a) and (d) describe the same tangent. In 9(viii), options (a) and (d) are equivalent. In 9(ix), a degenerate conic is possible without further restrictions. In 9(xii) the printed equation is an ellipse, although the stem calls it a hyperbola. These are retained as printed without an answer key. The reviewed portion then totalled 126.

## Review exercises 10–12

Differential Equations (8), Partial Differentiation (10), and Introduction to Numerical Methods (10) were transcribed from PDF pages 21–26 (printed pages 372–373, 386–387, and 407–408). All 154 MCQs in the supplied review-exercise extract are now represented. This does not by itself establish full textbook coverage; see the Unit 1 comparison below.

Printed issues retained without an answer key: 10(ii) shows an expression rather than a complete equation; 10(vi) does not specify a solution domain across the singular point x=0; 10(vii) also has the zero solution. In 11(i) and 11(iii), ratios require nonzero denominators; 11(vii) depends on how the trapezium is parameterized. In 12(i), a root in an interval does not determine a unique sign of the endpoint product; 12(iv) and 12(x) state “fastest” without conditions; 12(viii) and 12(ix) have overlapping choices. The exercise wording and choices remain as printed.
