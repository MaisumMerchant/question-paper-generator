# Class XII Physics MCQ source review

The supplied 21-page Physics MCQ extract contains 140 exercise questions, ten in each unit 15–28. Unit 16 (First Law of Thermodynamics) was restored; Unit 21 (Physics of Solids) was separated from Unit 22 (Solid State Electronics). Several options and nuclear notations were restored to match the source, including five choices for Unit 25 question 1. This extract does not establish coverage beyond its pages; no answer key is asserted.

Source issues retained rather than silently rewritten include:
- Unit 15 question 7 refers to average speed although the kinetic-theory formula uses mean square speed; some printed distractors have unclear notation.
- Unit 16 questions 9 and 10 lack conditions or include multiple equivalent expressions for an isochoric process.
- Unit 18 question 5 has multiple ways to increase a solenoid's field; Unit 20 question 8 has equivalent options, and question 7 does not correctly characterize resonance.
- Unit 25 question 10 lacks the term “work function” among its choices.
- Unit 26 questions 1 and 8 have overlapping valid choices; question 2 does not clearly specify the sign of the total energy.
- Unit 27 question 2 prints the electron notation with −1 as superscript and 0 as subscript; question 4 prints a placeholder nuclide rather than explicitly showing emitted particles. Both have been retained as printed.
