The Maths PDFs are named `Math_MCQs.pdf` while their banks are `Maths_MCQs.json`; the IX–XI Computer PDFs are `Computer_Science_MCQs.pdf` and their banks are `Computer_MCQs.json`. The site uses aliases for these existing pairings. General `.json` and `.pdf` files are not automatically evidence of a matching textbook MCQ exercise pair.

The Class IX Urdu Islamiat pair contains 35 exact source pages, verified by rendered-pixel comparison, and 101 visually checked transcriptions; it has not received an independent second proofread. The original draft is retained in `work-in-progress/stbb-265/`. The textbook is combined IX–X and no class division is asserted. Five questions have three source options; no fourth option or answer key has been invented. No Sindhi Islamiat MCQs are reintroduced.

Read each subject's review notes in this folder. Where notes report a source review, it covers the supplied extract, not necessarily all exercises in the full textbook. The repository-wide inventory is not a question-by-question audit of every bank, and the originals and page selections for other extracts have not all been independently re-verified in this audit. Find suitable official source textbooks and review their actual exercise pages before creating missing pairs; do not relabel past papers or general banks as textbook MCQs.
